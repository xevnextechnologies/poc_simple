## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the access, information and support required by Enervtek ("we") to carry out the pilot deployment of its machine monitoring system at Unit IV of Din Textile Mills Ltd ("the mill").

## 2. Summary of Requirements

| Requirement | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Site access for survey, machine selection and installation | Unit IV management |
| Technical consultation sessions | Power and Administration team, technicians |
| Access to existing machine data | Power and Administration team |
| Installation of metering equipment | Electrical team |
| Internet connectivity in the spinning hall | IT and Administration |
| A designated point of contact | Unit IV management |

## 3. Site Visits

We request access to Unit IV for the following visits. Visits will be scheduled at the mill's convenience.

### 3.1 Site Survey

Review of the electrical layout, distribution panels and existing metering.

### 3.2 Machine Selection

Identification, together with the mill's team, of the machines to be included in the pilot deployment.

### 3.3 Installation and Commissioning

Installation of the metering equipment and confirmation that data is being received.

## 4. Technical Consultation

We request short consultation sessions with the following teams.

### 4.1 Power and Administration Team

Discussion of the electrical setup, power sources and existing metering arrangements.

### 4.2 Technicians and Electricians

Identification of panel and cable locations, and support during installation.

## 5. Existing Data

Where the mill already records data related to its machines, we request access to it in its existing format. This includes:

- Machine downtime records
- Machine monitoring data
- Maintenance records
- Readings taken from machines through existing meters

## 6. Metering Equipment

We will install our metering equipment alongside the mill's existing systems.

- The equipment is clamp-on and can be removed without modification to existing installations.
- The equipment is read-only and does not control any machine or power source.
- Installation will be carried out by the mill's electricians, with our team present.

## 7. Connectivity

The metering equipment transmits readings through a data gateway, which requires internet access in the spinning hall by Wi-Fi or wired connection.

## 8. Data Confidentiality

All data collected during the pilot remains the property of Din Textile Mills Ltd. It will be stored securely, accessed only by our pilot team, and not shared with any third party.
