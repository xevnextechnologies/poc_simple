## Our request

We would like Din's permission to run a small measurement pilot in one department of your mill. The aim is to find out, together, whether machine-level data gives your team information it can act on.

We are not claiming any results. Every number in our demo is illustrative. The pilot exists to replace assumptions with measurements.

## What Enervtek does

Enervtek reads the electricity each machine draws and turns it into a clear picture of the floor:

- **When each machine runs, idles or stops,** and for how long, with the reason entered by staff.
- **What each machine and shift costs to run,** in units and rupees.
- **Which machines behave differently from their identical neighbours,** as a prompt for your team to inspect.

It works on any make or age of machine and only reads data. It never controls a machine. We built our demo from your mill's own electrical drawings, so the pilot starts from your layout, not a generic one.

## Why we are asking Din

Din Textile's FY2026 annual report names energy cost as one of the most significant challenges for the business. Fuel and power cost Rs 3.37 billion across the company, and spinning ran at about 81% of capacity, against 97% the year before.

Today, figures like these arrive as totals. We want to test whether measuring at machine level can show where the energy and the capacity actually go.

## What we hope to learn together

- How much running time is lost to stops, and to machines running light.
- What a machine, a shift and a kg of yarn cost in energy.
- Whether our automatic stop detection matches your team's own records.
- Whether the data gives your team at least one thing worth acting on.

## How we will work

- **Measure first, claim nothing.** Findings come only after a normal pattern has been recorded.
- **Din decides what success means.** Your team sets the criteria before we start, and judges the result at the end.
- **Small and reversible.** One department, removable equipment, no disruption to production.
- **Safe.** All electrical work is done by Din's electricians, with our engineer present.
- **Your data stays yours.** It remains confidential and is used only with Din's written permission.
- **Honest reporting.** If the data shows nothing useful, we will say so, and the pilot ends there.

## What we ask of Din

- Agreement to pilot in one department, chosen with your team.
- A contact person and a maintenance lead to work with.
- Access to the downtime records and production figures for that department.

We would welcome a conversation on which department would be most useful to start with.
